About HexGuard

Securing digital dreams, one audit at a time.

Our Mission

HexGuard was founded with a simple mission — make professional cybersecurity accessible to Indian small businesses and students. We believe security is not a luxury, it's a necessity.

Most cybersecurity firms charge enterprise rates that small businesses simply cannot afford. We bridge that gap with affordable, high-quality security assessments and practical training programs.

Our Approach

1

Understand

We learn about your business, technology stack, and security concerns.

2

Assess

We perform thorough testing using both automated and manual techniques.

3

Report

You get a clear, actionable report with prioritized fixes.

4

Fix & Follow Up

We help you implement fixes and re-test until secure.
